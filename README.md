# 🛒 Swiggy Instamart Sales Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive **Power BI sales analytics dashboard** built to explore sales performance, order trends, product categories, customer ratings, and outlet-level insights for Swiggy Instamart.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance and order volume.
- Identify top-performing product categories.
- Compare outlet sales across different city tiers and shop types.
- Understand average sales, item weight, and customer rating metrics.
- Explore outlet opening-year trends.
- Build an interactive dashboard to support data-driven business decisions.

## 📌 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Sales | 1.20M |
| Total Orders | 9K |
| Average Rating | 3.92 |
| Average Item Weight | 12.86 |
| Average Sales | 141.01 |

*Values are displayed as rounded KPIs where applicable.*

## 📈 Key Business Insights

### 1. Sales Performance
- Total sales are approximately **1.20 million**.
- The average sales value is **141.01**.
- Outlet and product-level comparisons help identify important contributors to overall revenue.

### 2. Product Category Analysis
- **Fruits and Vegetables** is the leading category, with approximately 178.2K in sales.
- **Snack Foods** follows closely, with approximately 175.4K.
- Household products contribute approximately 136.0K.
- Frozen Foods, Dairy, and Canned products are also significant contributors.

### 3. Shop City Tier Analysis
The dashboard compares total sales and order distribution across three city tiers.

| Shop City Tier | Total Sales |
|---|---:|
| Tier 3 | 472K |
| Tier 2 | 393K |
| Tier 1 | 336K |

**Insight:** Tier 3 outlets contribute the highest sales among the three city tiers shown.

### 4. Shop Type Analysis
The dashboard compares Grocery Store and Supermarket outlet types using total sales, total orders, average rating, and average sales.

- Supermarket Type1 contributes the largest share of sales among the displayed shop types.
- Shop-type comparisons help evaluate differences in sales and order performance.
- Average ratings across the displayed shop types are relatively consistent, around 3.91–3.93.

### 5. Outlet Opening-Year Analysis
The dashboard includes a year-wise view of shop city type counts, allowing users to explore outlet distribution across opening years and identify differences between years.

### 6. Customer Ratings
The overall average rating is **3.92**, providing a high-level view of customer feedback across the outlets represented in the dataset.

## 🛠️ Tools and Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI Desktop** — Dashboard development and visualization
- **Power Query** — Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** — KPI calculations and measures
- **Data Modeling** — Organizing data for analysis
- **GitHub** — Project documentation and version control

## 🎛️ Dashboard Features

- Interactive slicers for shop opening year, shop city tier, and shop size
- KPI cards for sales, orders, average rating, and average item weight
- Donut charts for order and average sales comparisons
- Bar charts for category sales and city-tier comparisons
- Year-wise trend visualization
- Detailed table for shop-type and category-level metrics
- Cross-filtering for interactive data exploration

## 🚀 How to Explore the Project

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open the `.pbix` file in Microsoft Power BI Desktop.
3. Interact with the slicers to filter the report by opening year, city tier, and shop size.
4. Compare category sales, shop-type performance, orders, and average ratings.
5. Use the dashboard visuals to explore business performance across different segments.

## 💡 Business Value

This project demonstrates how business intelligence and interactive data visualization can transform retail sales data into actionable insights. It helps users compare outlet performance, identify leading product categories, and explore sales patterns across multiple business dimensions.
